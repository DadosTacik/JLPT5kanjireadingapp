# Privacy Policy for JLPT5 Reading App

**Effective date: August 30, 2026**

## 1. Publisher and scope

This Privacy Policy applies to the JLPT5 Reading App, an educational application for learning Japanese reading and JLPT N5 vocabulary.

The application is published by:

**Dominik Rybka**  
Poland

Privacy contact:

**dadostacik@gmail.com**

## 2. No collection of personal information

JLPT5 Reading App is designed to work fully offline.

The Publisher does not collect, receive, transmit, sell, share, or otherwise process personal information through the application.

The application does not require:

- a user account;
- registration or sign-in;
- a name or email address;
- identification of the user;
- an internet connection for its learning functionality.

The application does not use analytics, advertising, behavioral tracking, authentication services, payment services, or cloud storage for learning information.

## 3. Information stored locally

To provide its learning functionality, the application stores certain information locally on the user’s device.

This may include:

- learning progress;
- completed lessons;
- saved flashcards;
- selected translation language;
- onboarding status;
- the last lesson position;
- study timer information;
- other application preferences.

This information is used only by the application on the user’s device.

It is not:

- transmitted to the Publisher;
- uploaded to a developer server;
- used for advertising;
- used for analytics;
- shared with other companies or organizations;
- used to identify the user.

The Publisher cannot remotely access this locally stored information.

## 4. Data retention and deletion

Locally stored information remains on the device until it is removed by the user or by the device’s operating system.

Users can remove this information by:

- clearing the application’s storage in the device settings; or
- uninstalling the application.

The device’s operating system may include application information in system-level backups, depending on the user’s device and backup settings. Such backups are controlled by the operating-system or backup-service provider. The Publisher does not have access to them.

Because the application does not create user accounts and does not collect information on a developer server, there is no server-side user account or personal information for the Publisher to delete.

## 5. Device features and permissions

The application’s learning functionality does not use the user’s location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos, calendar, or advertising identifier to collect personal information.

The application may use standard device features such as vibration or haptic feedback.

The application does not require access to location, audio recording, photos, camera, or device storage.

## 6. Text-to-speech functionality

The application may use the text-to-speech service installed on the user’s device to play Japanese pronunciation.

The Publisher does not receive the spoken text, voice information, or other information processed by the device’s text-to-speech service.

Text-to-speech functionality may be provided by the device manufacturer, operating-system provider, or another text-to-speech service selected by the user. Its operation is governed by the settings and privacy policy of that provider.

## 7. Third-party services

The application does not integrate third-party analytics, advertising networks, social-media tracking, cloud databases, authentication providers, or payment processors.

Google Play, the Apple App Store, the device operating system, and the device manufacturer may independently process technical information when the application is downloaded, installed, updated, backed up, or operated.

Those services are controlled by their respective providers and are governed by their own privacy policies. Their processing is not performed by the Publisher through JLPT5 Reading App.

## 8. Hosting of this Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy is hosted using GitHub Pages.

When a person visits the Privacy Policy website, GitHub may process standard technical information required to deliver the webpage, such as an IP address or browser information. This processing is performed independently by GitHub and is governed by GitHub’s own privacy statement.

Visiting the Privacy Policy website is separate from using the offline JLPT5 Reading App.

## 9. Children’s privacy

The application does not knowingly collect personal information from children or adults.

The application does not create online profiles, identify users, or transmit learning activity to the Publisher.

Parents or guardians should supervise the use of the application when appropriate.

## 10. Security

Because the application does not transmit personal information to a developer server, the Publisher does not maintain a server-side database containing user information.

Locally stored information is protected by the security of the user’s device. Users should use an appropriate screen lock and keep their operating system up to date.

## 11. Changes to this Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the application’s functionality or privacy practices change.

Any updated version will be published at the same public address, and the effective date shown at the top of the document will be revised.
